Nobody has to run it

Every message in that chat log is the bot acting on its own. You don't start a bet, watch a clock, tally anything up or pay anyone out — and none of it needs you to be live.

30 minutes before kickoff Bets open by themselves

The bot is already watching the schedule. It takes the sportsbook line, locks it so it can't move, and posts the announcement in chat. Nothing triggers this but the clock.

The half hour that follows Picks are taken and confirmed instantly

Each !pick is checked against that person's real balance, the coins come out, and the bot replies. Not enough ZCoins, a second pick on the same game, a typo'd team — it says so and takes nothing.

At kickoff Betting closes by itself

A last call goes out at five minutes. At kickoff the market shuts and anything typed after that gets told it's too late. No one can sneak a pick in once the game is underway.

After the final whistle Winners are paid without anyone asking

The bot waits for the official final score, grades every pick against the line it locked, pays the winners into their real balance and posts the results with a link to the full breakdown.

The one thing that isn't instant: settlement waits on the official final score, which usually lands within about ten minutes of full time. The bot posts the moment it has it — whether that's straight away or an hour later, and whether or not you're still streaming.

Nothing you already have changes. !points, !top, !duel, !roulette and the rest are StreamElements' own commands and keep working exactly as they do today — Picks doesn't touch or replace any of them.

One thing to check: StreamElements has its own !bet command for its built-in betting contests, which is why these use !pick instead. If you'd rather chat typed !bet, that's fine — it just means turning off the StreamElements betting module first so the two aren't fighting over the same word.

What happens to your ZCoins

The stake comes out when the bet is placed

jimmytomato's 100 leaves his balance the moment he types it, so nobody can bet points they've already spent somewhere else.

Winners get paid the moment the game is graded

He gets his 100 back plus 67 profit — 167 in total. A losing Pick gets nothing back, so those ZCoins are gone: bet 50 out of 100 and you're left with 50 whether you wait for the result or not.

Everything else keeps working exactly as it does now

Raffles, duels, giveaways and watchtime are untouched. Someone can win a raffle in the middle of a bet and both land correctly — Picks only ever adds or subtracts, it never overwrites anyone's balance.

One honest note: slightly more points go out than come in over time, because payouts round up in the viewer's favour and there's no house taking a cut. At the scale of your chat that's small, and it's a dial we can turn if it ever drifts.
