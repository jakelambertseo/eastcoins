East Arena

This is the Lounge, where everyone in the arena hangs out. The door on the east wall leads into the campaign: Act 1 · The Cellars, three areas of four rooms each, packs in every room and the Hoodie at the end of the third. Walk to the door and press E. Click another player to invite them to a party (up to four).

Your loot is yours alone: walk over it or click its name. It goes to your inventory when you leave the area, but if you die there, what you found there is lost. A first clear gives one perk, kept all season. Each level gives a skill point.
