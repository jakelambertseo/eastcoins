Dungeon reports: a sketch

Design only. Nothing here is wired to the game, and every number is made up. Three pieces: the live meter a party watches while it fights, the after-action report that opens when a run ends, and how both sit on a phone. Click the meter's modes and the report's column headings; they work.

Where it sits

Top right, under the minimap, in the HUD's dark glass rather than the paper windows: it is a gauge you glance at mid-fight, not a window you read. Drag it anywhere; the ▾ collapses it to names and numbers.

Four modes

Damage (and damage per second), Taken, Food (every fish eaten, as the HP it gave back), and Deaths. The bar colour is your fighting style: red melee, green archery, blue magic. Your own bar wears a gold ring.

Two windows of time

Fight resets when a boss or a floor starts, so each boss has its own leaderboard; Run is the whole dungeon. Details! players will know it as "current segment / overall".

What it costs

Today a run only keeps damage to the boss (and the Count Room's kills). The meter and the report want a handful of counters per player per run: damage to anything (storm arcs and burns included), damage taken, food eaten by kind, deaths, kills, XP by skill, hits and misses. All of them sit on code paths that already run on every swing and bite, so it is additions, not new systems. They reach the page with the updates the party already gets, a couple of times a second at most. No new poll.

The order it reads in

The result and the grade first (did we do it, how well), then the awards (the fun, screenshot-able part), then the scoreboard, then the detail: the damage over time with the bosses marked, your XP, the loot, and who went down and why.

Awards, not just rankings

A table only ever praises the top damage. Awards find something for everyone: most fish eaten, took the most hits, never died, landed the killing blow, most accurate. Every one is computed from the same counters.

The grade

S / A / B / C from time, deaths and damage taken against the dungeon's par. It gives a cleared run something to beat next time, and your best grade can sit on the Hiscores and your profile.

Share it

"Post to chat" drops a one-line summary in chat: "The Crypt cleared in 11:42, grade A. MVP: Kellz (39% of the damage)." Your party's, not a stranger's: only the people in the run get the window.

A wipe still gets a report

That is when people most want one. The headline says how far you got and the boss's HP when it ended, the grade is replaced by "how close", and the deaths card moves to the top: who went first, to what, and what they had left to eat.

No blame colours

Deaths are listed, not shamed: no red rows. The awards still run ("Last one standing", "Most fish eaten"), so a bad night still hands out something.

The meter on a phone

Collapsed by default: names and numbers only, no mode strip. Tap it to open the full meter over the top of the screen; it never sits over the chat drawer.

The report on a phone

The table becomes one card per player (four numbers each), the awards go two across, and the chart and loot stack below. The same data, nothing dropped.
