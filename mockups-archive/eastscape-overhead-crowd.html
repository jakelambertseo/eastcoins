Five monsters, one screen

The Thunderhead at the game's own scale: a Thunder Goose, two Thunderwolves, a Storm Golem and a Hail Drake, a tile or two apart, and you. Move the mouse over one to hover it, click to target it, then hit.

The three ways

  • 1 · Every plate, as is. What you'd get by just turning plates on for everything: on a small map they sit on top of each other and nothing is readable.
  • 2 · Every plate, stacked. Plates push each other upward until nothing overlaps, with a thin line down to whoever owns it (how Final Fantasy XIV and Guild Wars 2 handle a crowd). Everything readable, but it's a tower of boxes and it jumps about as monsters walk.
  • 3 · Focus (recommended). The full plate, with name, number and icons, only on what you've targeted and what's under your mouse. Everything else gets a slim bar with its number, and only once it's hurt, so a pack at full health is just monsters. The weaknesses you care about are the ones on the thing you're hitting; hover any other to read it. Stacking still applies to the two full plates so they never cover each other.
