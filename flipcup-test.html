How it goes

  • Drink: ten presses empties the cup. Faster is faster.
  • Flip: press once while the marker is in the green. Too soft and it flops; too hard and it's on the floor. Miss and you go again.
  • The green gets smaller and the marker quicker with every cup. The anchor's is nasty.
