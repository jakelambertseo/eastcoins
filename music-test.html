What moved, and why

  • "Now playing" lives on the video. Title, requester and their tier sit in an overlay at the bottom of the stage, with the progress bar as the stage's bottom edge. The separate Now Playing card goes away.
  • One control bar. Reactions (good on the left, bad on the right, a divider between), Skip vote and volume in a single row under the stage instead of three places.
  • One request box. The search field becomes the page's one big input, with a hint line for the chat commands beneath it. Results drop down from it.
  • "In the room" folds into the header. A stacked pile of avatars and a count next to the title, with See who for the full list. It no longer takes a panel.
  • One rail, three tabs. Up next, History, Rankings. Top Requesters merges into Rankings as a second sort, so ELO and request counts sit on the same rows rather than in two blocks.
  • Queue rows say when. An ETA per song ("in 10 min") and a YOU tag on your own requests, with a ✕ to pull them.

Small things worth adding while we're in there

  • Keyboard: space nothing (video owns it), 1–4 react, S skip vote, / focus the request box.
  • After you rate, a tiny +3 / −2 next to the requester's rating so ELO feels earned.
  • A "now playing" chip in the nav on other pages, so leaving the Green Room doesn't mean losing track of the song.
  • Queue cap per person shown inline ("2 of 3 in queue") instead of an error after the fact.
