How to play

  • Drag back from anywhere, like a slingshot. Longer pull, more power.
  • The wind changes every turn. Check it before you pull.
  • Your last shot stays drawn as a dotted line. Adjust from it.
  • Head 60 · body 30 · legs 20. First to empty the other's 100 wins.
