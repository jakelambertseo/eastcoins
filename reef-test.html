    How the real one works

    The server rolls every bite from the spot, your rod and the real time and season in Chicago, so rare fish can't be picked.

    It keeps your tank and when you last collected, and works out Pearls when you open the page. Nothing polls. Pearls never turn into ZCoins; it plays for titles.

    Skills

    Everything you do trains a skill, from level 1 to 99 on the RuneScape XP curve. Early levels come fast; 99 is a long road.
