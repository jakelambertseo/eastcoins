    How the real one works

    The server rolls every bite from the spot, your rod and the reef's own clock, so rare fish can't be picked. The reef runs a 24-minute day and a two-day season, and its weather is a pure function of that clock — so everyone is in the same storm at the same moment, and any past or future hour can be worked out without having stored it.

    It keeps your tank and when you last collected, and works out Pearls when you open the page. Nothing polls. Pearls never turn into ZCoins; it plays for titles.

    Leviathans

    One monster lives at every spot. They only bite for someone who knows the water, and they fight on a clock.

    Inventory

    Skills

    Everything you do trains a skill, from level 1 to 99 on the RuneScape XP curve. Early levels come fast; 99 is a long road.
