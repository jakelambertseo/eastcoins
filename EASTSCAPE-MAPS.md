## 2. Where it thins

**Combat 72–80 is a hole.** After the Grinning Man (72) and the House (70) the next outdoor monster is a
junkdog at **80**. The only 73–79 anything is the Vault's warden (74), and the Vault is an interior with three
monsters and a party feel. A player at 74 has nowhere to stand. This is the "in-between for the junkyard" —
it belongs between the Thunderhead and the Trailer Park, and it is the most urgent thing on this page.

**Fishing 62 → 80.** Goldfish/koi (62/73, the Carnival's duck pond) then mud cat/bowfin (80/75). Cooking
follows it exactly: koi cooks at 73, then nothing until mud cat at 65 (out of order: the cook levels for
mud cat 65 / bowfin 75 sit BELOW the fishing levels 80/75 that catch them). A 70s fish is missing.

**Woodcutting 50 → 65.** Skyash at 45/50, then pine at 65. Nothing in between; and pine, bogwood and
gallows are the whole top of the ladder (65, 80, 90).

**Mining 60 → 90 lives in one room.** Starfall (60) is outdoors in three places; eclipse (70) and nova (80)
are Vault-only; singularity (90) is Trailer Park-only. Two full gear tiers are mined in a single interior.

**Thieving 62 → 75 → 90** are three marks in one guild room. **Alchemy** has nothing between 90 and the two
level-100 potions (dead until the cap moves). **Agility** is one course, no ladder.

**Quests thin out exactly where players are arriving.** The Carnival has one, the Vault three, the Trailer
Park three, the wild maps none. Twenty-two of the forty-five are in the Yard, Gloam, Mire and Boneyard.

**Exclusive drops.** Every wild-only material now feeds a recipe (wild silk, marrow, grimcore). Nothing the
Carnival or the Vault drops is exclusive to a recipe path; their rares are the same shared pool.

## 3. The in-between map: THE SCRAP LINE (combat 72–80)

Where the junk comes from before it is a junkyard: the county's pull-a-part lot, a mile of dead cars on
cinder blocks, a crusher at the far end, a runoff pond nobody should fish and everybody will. Sits **north
of the Thunderhead, south of the Trailer Park** — the Trailer Park's exit moves one map further out, so
the road that goes 70 → 80 today goes 70 → 74 → 80.

**Monsters (five, one boss), all aggressive at the crusher end:**
- **Lot Dog** 73 — a junkdog's little brother; drops bones, feathers off the birds it ate, tyre rubber.
- **Magpie** 75 — a flying thief: hits and TAKES a stack of tickets the first time (returned on kill, like a
  pickpocket in reverse); drops shiny things: gems (ruby/sapphire/topaz), 1-in-40 an opal.
- **Tire Fire** 76 — a living tyre fire, weak to frost, strong to fire; drops charcoal (8–12!) and
  **rubber**, the new material.
- **Crusher Hand** 78 — the man who runs the crusher; drops eclipse-grade parts: **crushed plate**.
- **Oil Slick** 79 — a slime; drops **sump oil** (the alchemy input this band lacks).
- **THE COMPACTOR** 82 (boss, hourly like the Critic's placement, not the King's clock) — drops nova ore
  (the first outside the Vault), the **crusher's badge** (a +tix amulet), 1-in-60 a **Scrap Pup** pet.

**Nodes:** **eclipse ore ×3 (70)** and **nova ore ×1 (80)** outdoors at last, behind the Compactor; a
**pallet pile** (woodcutting 58 → "pallet wood": the missing 50–65 rung, shafts at 60, paper at 58); the
**runoff pond** (fishing 70: **drainpike** 70 / **oil carp** 76, cooking 70 / 78 — fills the 62–80 fishing
hole and puts cooking back in order); a **battery rack** for thieving (mark 70: sump oil, wire, tickets).

**Recipes it feeds:** rubber + bogwood → **tyre boots** (nova-tier boots with +speed, the one outdoor
nova piece); sump oil + large vial → **pot_slick** (Alchemy 74: -20% mob accuracy, 10 min); crushed plate
×3 + eclipse bar → eclipse gear at the anvil at a discount (the Vault stays the fast route, the Scrap Line
the slow free one); drainpike → smoked 74.

**Quests (three, from one NPC, "Ma Belcher" who runs the lot): easy** bring 10 rubber; **medium** kill 8
Magpies and bring the tickets they took back to her; **hard** kill the Compactor with a bow (she wants it
done from a distance).

**Cost:** ~7 monster sprites + 1 boss + 1 NPC (two facings) + ~10 icons + a 44×26 map on the Trailer
Park's tiles (rustpine, wreck, tyres, dumpster already exist) — about the Wilderness's art bill, which was
one evening plus the map itself.

## 4. Four more maps — ideas only, in the order I would build them

### A. The Foundry (mining / smithing 60–99, combat 76–86)
An abandoned smelter under the Thunderhead's cliffs, reached by a lift on the Thunderhead's west edge.
Eclipse ore ×4 (70), nova ×3 (80), **the second singularity source (90)**, and a **blast furnace** that
smelts two bars a charge (the Nexus idea for smithing). Monsters: **Slag Golem** 76, **Furnace Imp** 78,
**Foreman** 84 (a human, thieving mark too), boss **The Bessemer** 88 (a walking furnace; drops
**tempered plate** → the Hallowed-style "+4% tickets" perk on nova gear). Fills the Vault-only gap for
mining without touching the Vault. Also the natural home for a **Smithing 99 tier** when the cap moves.

### B. The Boardwalk (fishing / cooking 60–90, combat 66–78)
A drowned seaside boardwalk east of the Carnival (the Carnival's back fence opens). Piers hold
**mackerel** 60 / **bluefin** 72 / **swordfish** 84, a **crab pot** mechanic (set it, come back in an hour
— Harvesting's timer on the water), and a **chip shop** range that cooks two at once. Monsters: **Gull**
66 (feathers, the 70s bird archery needs), **Boardwalk Drunk** 69, **Pier Shark** 74, **Kraken Arm** 78
(rises from the water at night, the Nightfall idea reused), boss **The Ferris** 80 (a possessed wheel).
Fills fishing 62–80 AND gives cooking its top rung outside the Deep Wild.

### C. The Orchard Wall (woodcutting / harvesting / fletching 50–80, combat 58–70)
A walled orchard gone feral between the Boneyard and the Sands. **Cherry** 52 / **walnut** 66 trees, a
**hedge maze** (agility course #2 — the second course the skill needs, timed laps like The Run), wild
**crop beds** (harvesting 55–75 without an island: beetroot, hops → a brewer NPC → **ale** meals), and the
**Beekeeper's** hives (honey: a cooking input, the first +heal-over-time meal). Monsters: **Wasp** 58,
**Scarecrow** 62 (weak to fire), **Orchard Keeper** 66, **Hedge Thing** 70, boss **The Gardener** 72.
Fletching gets walnut shortbows (68) between skyash and pine.

### D. The Deep Vault / Sub-Basement (late game 88–99, a party interior)
The floor UNDER the Vault: the count room's count room. Reached only with a **Dealer's key** (a rare from
the Dealer 92 — gives the Vault a reason at 92). Three rooms of 88–96 monsters (**Auditor's Shade** 88,
**Vault Golem** 91, **Ledger Wraith** 94) and a boss **The Accountant** 99 who drops the first
**level-100 materials** for when the cap moves (the two dead Alchemy recipes are waiting for exactly this).
Singularity ore ×2 inside. Thieving 90+ marks (the ringleader's boss). This is the "what do I do at 92"
answer, and the Crypt's party code carries it almost whole.

## 5. Order and why

1. **The Scrap Line** — closes the 72–80 hole that people are hitting NOW; small map, existing tile set.
2. **The Boardwalk** — the fishing/cooking hole is the next one anyone at 65 feels.
3. **The Foundry** — mining out of the Vault; sets up the 99 tier.
4. **The Orchard Wall** — the skilling middle (woodcutting 50–65, agility #2, harvesting off-island).
5. **The Deep Vault** — last, because it is only for the top ten players until the cap moves to 120.

Each new map is one `SCENES` entry (or a closed-file map like the wild ones), its mobs in `MOBS`/`LOOT`/
`BOUNTY`/`WEAK`, its nodes in `build()`, an `AREA_ART` list and an `ART_FILES` block, an area icon for the
wiki, and `tools/eastscape-balance.mjs` run before the numbers are trusted.
