Eight golf carts, one cliff, and fog so thick nobody knows where the edge is. The carts only get faster. Hit the brakes once: closest to the edge without going over takes the round. Five rounds. No ZCoins, only nerve.

How it scores

  • Stop safely and you score how far along you got: 93% of the way to the edge is 93 points.
  • Closest safe cart gets +25.
  • Over the edge is zero, and a long way down.
  • The fog only shows the last few yards. Early on you can react to it. At full speed you can't: that's the game.
