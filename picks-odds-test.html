How EastCoin Consensus Odds Work

We do not average American moneyline numbers directly.

1Read each US bookmaker's home and away h2h prices.
2Convert each price to implied probability.
3Remove that bookmaker's vig by normalizing its two probabilities to 100%.
4Take the median fair probability across bookmakers for each team.
5Normalize the median pair and convert it back to American odds.
Fixed payout +A: return = wager × (1 + A/100) -A: return = wager × (1 + 100/A) Test payouts are floored to whole ZCoins.
