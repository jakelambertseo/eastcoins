THE LOUNGE

Everyone's front door to the games. Hang out, see who's here and where everyone else is playing, read the high scores, change your look at the prize counter, and walk up to a cabinet to play. The Climb's cabinet works. Signed in to eastcoin.vip you arrive as yourself; with no room server running, bots stand in for the people.

How it works, and what it costs

The lounge is a way in, never in the way: a direct link to a game skips it. The plan is to launch the lounge and The Climb together.

LoadingThe lounge is its own page. A cabinet is a link, so nothing from the lounge runs while you play. Three.js, the characters and the models are cached for a year, so after the first visit a cabinet costs about half a second (building the scene), not a download.
Lag in the loungeOne live room per ~40 people (a Durable Object, like EastScape's), positions about 10 a second and only for people near you, chat on the same socket. A full lounge opens a second one. The glow is one bloom pass; low-end machines can switch it off.
Notifications"Your record fell", "drhealsgud rang the bell": no new polling. They ride the bell's existing request (/api/picks/notifications), the way the Gold Button does; in the lounge the room's own socket carries them too.
High scoresOne game_scores table for every game (game, user, metric, value, season), written by each game's server when a run is checked, read by the lounge's board, profiles and Wrapped. Reads are cached at the edge.
Your lookOne record of character and hat that every game reads (here it is ecPlayer in this browser). Hats are earned in the games (the crown at the top of The Climb) or bought with ZCoins in the existing store.
Hanging outWho's online and where ("The Climb · floor 61") with a Join button, lounge chat, the board to argue about, and things to stand round: air hockey (two players), the claw, three tables. Later: friends, parties walking up to a cabinet together, emotes.
