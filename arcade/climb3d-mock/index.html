A 100-floor dungeon tower, climbed together. The ledges are on the walls; the middle is a long way down. Miss a jump and you land wherever you land: a floor below, or ten. Every tenth floor is a landing room whose stone floor catches you, and its brazier saves your place. Built so far: The Cellar (floors 1–10, plain jumping) , The Barracks (11–20: narrow beams, and gaps only the dive crosses), The Well (21–30: chimneys you climb by kicking between two glowing walls), The Forge (31–40: brass platforms to ride across, fire vents to time), The Crypt (41–50: slabs that crumble under you, in the dark), The Clockworks (51–60: turntables, clock hands and conveyor belts), The Frost Vault (61–70: ice that won't let you stop, and falling icicles), The Gale Gallery (71–80: spring pads, and gusts that blow you off the walls), The Chain Hall (81–90: swings you ride and let go of, and maces) and The Storm Spire (91–100: everything at once, and lightning). At the top: the roof, the bell, the crown.
