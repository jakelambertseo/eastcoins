Shells are the Blockshot currency: you earn them by playing and spend them on cases. ZCoins can buy the Knife Case too, so the site's money has a way in without being the only way. Skins change how a gun, a knife or your bean looks and nothing else. Everything is a one-off: a skin found twice turns into Shells.
