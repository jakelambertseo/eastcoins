What's in it

One matchAlways running on the arcade server. You take a slot from a bot, play the round in progress, and a bot takes the slot back when you leave. 12 slots. The server owns movement, shots, kills and the clock; your own bean is predicted so it feels instant, and shots are judged against where everyone was when you fired.
Krunker's gunsThe numbers are Krunker's own: the assault rifle 23 a hit every 130 ms, 28 rounds; the sniper 109 (163 to the head) once a second, 3 rounds, 2.7× scope; the shotgun five pellets of 50 every 450 ms, 2 shells. Health regenerates after five seconds without being hit.
Maps for slidingThe Lot and The Docks: ramps instead of stairs, big cover you go round, low ledges you step onto without breaking a slide, jump pads to the roofs. Shift slides, Space hops out keeping the speed; slide again as you land for more.
Levels, skins, statsA kill is 10 XP (headshot 15), a win 100, a round 40. Levels unlock body colours, patterns, visors and gun finishes in the Locker. Lifetime kills, K/D, accuracy, headshots, wins, streaks, by gun and by map. Signed-in players keep theirs; guests play but don't.
The HUDHealth bottom left in ten segments (it pulses when you're low), ammo bottom right with the reload sweep, the clock up top, the feed on the right. The crosshair opens as you move and in the air and closes when you stand still. A red arc shows where the hit came from. Nothing sits in the middle of the screen.
NextStats and an all-time board in the database per Twitch account, "N playing" on the Games card, spectating, a map vote, mastery per gun.
