What's in it

Maps for slidingThe Lot (an open plaza, a raised centre with a ramp on every side, four roofs with long ramps and bridges) and The Docks (three long lanes, two warehouses with ramps at both ends, a catwalk, piers). Ramps instead of stairs, big cover you go round, low ledges you step onto without breaking a slide, and jump pads that fly you to the roofs. Each map is a list of boxes, one merged mesh per texture.
Levels and skinsA kill is 10 XP (a headshot 15), a win 100, a round 40, every streak of three 20. Level n needs 100×n^1.5 more; level 10 is a few evenings. Levels unlock body colours, patterns, visors and gun finishes in the Locker (Solid Gold at 25, a gold gun at 30) and a title (Recruit → Immortal). Nothing is bought.
StatsLifetime kills, deaths, K/D, accuracy, headshots and headshot rate, wins and win rate, rounds, best streak, time played, and the same by gun and by map. In this browser for now; on the server, the server counts every kill and they follow your Twitch account.
Sounds and texturesAll drawn and synthesised in code: concrete, brick, metal, grass, crate, hazard stripes and the skin patterns; gunshots, hits, headshots, kills, streaks, reloads, pads, the countdown, level-ups. No files to download, nothing to license. A real pack can replace any of them one at a time.
SettingsMouse sensitivity, field of view, volume, invert Y, crosshair style. Saved in the browser.
