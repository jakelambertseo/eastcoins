How it works

No-limit Texas Hold'em, blinds 5 and 10, six seats, real people only. Buy in for 200 to 1,000 chips from your bank and leave whenever a hand is not running.

Chips are ZCoins. 1 ZC buys 200 chips. Open the bank (top bar) to buy chips with ZCoins or cash chips out; a seat is bought from your chip bank first, then ZCoins. The house takes nothing: every chip on the table came from a player and goes back to one. You can put in 20 ZC a day.

Thirty seconds a decision. Run out the clock and you check or fold; twice in a row and you are sat out until you come back. Keys: F fold, C check or call, R bet or raise. While you wait, Check / Fold and Call any act for you the moment it is your turn. Raise sizes land on round numbers.

Leaving mid-hand folds you. Lose your connection and the seat is held a minute, then your chips go back to the bank.

The dealer is the server. Nobody's page is told a card it may not see.
