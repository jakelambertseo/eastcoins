How it works

No-limit Texas Hold'em, blinds 1 and 2, six seats, real people only. Buy in for 50 to 200 chips from your bank and leave whenever a hand is not running.

Chips are play chips for now. Everyone starts with 1,000 in the bank; if you go broke and leave the table, the bank tops you back up once an hour. When the table goes live, chips will be bought with ZCoins at the table's rate, so a 1 ZC player and a 1,000 ZC player can sit at the same table.

Twenty seconds a decision. Run out the clock and you check or fold; twice in a row and you are sat out until you come back.

Leaving mid-hand folds you. Lose your connection and the seat is held a minute, then your chips go back to the bank.

The dealer is the server. Nobody's page is told a card it may not see.
