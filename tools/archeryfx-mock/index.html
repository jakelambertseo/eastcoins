Archery Feel

Magic already looks and sounds like magic: every element has its own flight, its own impact animation and its own sound, plus burns, arcs and a flash when a page is read. A bow fires a small picture of the arrow and throws a few brown splinters. The numbers are fair (archery has the highest plain damage), but it doesn't feel like it. Below: what a shot does today, then a dozen changes that make every arrow land, all page-only, with no change to the rules or the server.

Sounds

Today's three archery sounds are good recordings; there just aren't enough of them. The proposal is about a dozen more, the same way magic got a set per element. In this mockup the new ones are synthesized stand-ins; in the game they'd be recordings like the rest.

Building it

  • Page only. The server already sends everything needed: who shot whom, the arrow (ak), hit or miss, crit, kill, weak or resist. Nothing about the result changes; it's how a decided hit looks and sounds.
  • Where it goes: the arrow drawing loop and the splat handler's land() in eastscape.html (where the splinters are thrown today), plus a draw pose on the archer's sprite.
  • Cost to run: a few more particles per shot from the pool that splinters already use, and up to three stuck arrows per monster. All of it stops under "calm" (reduced motion), like spells.
  • Art: the stuck arrow is the arrow's own icon, cropped. Gem trails and the bullseye ring are drawn, not painted. No new PixelLab art strictly needed; the impact bursts could later get sprite sheets like the spells have.
  • Sounds: about a dozen new takes (two twangs, five materials, a ping, a miss thud, four gem tails, a tick), sourced the way the spell recordings were.
  • Size: about two days for the draw, trail, impact, stuck arrows, bullseye and miss; another day for gem arrows, materials and the kits on the archer; the sounds once they're sourced.
