⚠️ The short version

Your hunch holds on every number the game keeps. The Wild is a place people pass through or skill in, not a place they fight. Its monsters are no longer a threat to the top of the server, and dying there costs less than dying anywhere else.

📈 The monsters against the players

Each monster in the Wild (purple) and the Deep (red) sits at its level; a ring round it means at least one of them attacks on sight. The dots underneath are every active character by combat level. The fighting in both maps is set for combat 20 to 65; the top of the server is 71 to 91.

🐺 Every monster, fought

For each level: swings to kill it, the seconds that takes, the health it takes off you in that time, and how many minutes it would need to kill you if you stood there and ignored it with no food. Green means it can't hurt you, amber is a nuisance, red is a real fight. Every monster lands at least 18% of its swings (the game's hit floor), which is why even a Bog Gnasher can in theory kill a level 90 in five minutes. In practice it dies in one swing.

⛏️ Why people go

Everything worth clicking in both maps, and how much of it the whole server has gathered since launch. Two rules apply in both maps. Gathering runs at half speed (WILD_GATHER 0.5), and the Deep gives 1.5× xp for gathering only (not for fighting), so a Deep node nets 0.75× the xp of the same node elsewhere. The Deep's two stations, the Nexus altar and the Wild Bench, are not slowed and print 1.5× for 1.25× xp.

🧍 Who goes, and what they did

Every character with any activity. A Wild-only kill is a monster that lives nowhere else (Wild Weaver, Marrow Hound, Grim Lich). The rest (revenants, wolves, drakes, golems and so on) also live on safe maps, so their kills can't be split by where they happened. Deaths in the Wild count monsters and players together.
