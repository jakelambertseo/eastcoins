What makes a good sink

Wanted, not takenThe sinks players love are things they want: a luxury, a gamble, a trophy, a convenience. Taxes and decay take the same tickets and make people play less. Every idea below is something you'd choose to buy.
Priced by the hourA regular earns about 17k tickets an hour. A flat 500-ticket fee is half an hour for someone on day one and nothing to a saver on 1.8M. Price sinks in hours of play for the middle, and let the top ones scale: a price that rises each time, an auction, or a top shelf priced in millions.
Again, not onceMost of today's sinks are bought once (gear, vanity, projects). A rich player runs out of things to buy and the tickets just sit. Rerolls, consumables, upkeep and weekly auctions come back.
Seen by othersWhat makes the rich spend is other people seeing it: a name in the CASINO line, a statue, a title, a trophy, a gold thing in the Yard. Prestige is the cheapest thing a game can sell.
Never touching a betThe house rule stands: nothing here changes odds or a ZCoin payout. Ticket bets already convert tickets into ZCoin chances under their own hourly cap; sinks sit beside that, not inside it.
MeasuredTickets in vs out per day, the total held, the median and the top-10 share are already tracked. A sink is working if the total held stops climbing while the median doesn't fall.
