Mythic runs

The Crypt and the Great Pyramid get Mythic levels: the same maps and the same bosses, a little harder every level with no ceiling, a clock to beat, and a set of twists that change every Thursday. Beat the clock and your key goes up; miss it and it goes down. Somewhere past Mythic 15 the monsters are level 100-plus and the runs need a healer and a tank, which is what mockup 2's Mender and Juggernaut were for. The Tower gets its own solo version, and everything pays out in the things mockup 3 needs.

How it works

Small rules, and one number to chase: your key for each dungeon.

Clear the top tierBeat the Black Crypt or the Great Pyramid once and you're handed a Mythic 1 key for it.
→
Run your keyThe party runs one member's key. Any level up to the key works, so you can run lower with a friend.
→
Beat the clockIn time: the key goes +1, under 80% of the time +2, under 60% +3. Everyone in the party gets theirs raised to match.
→
Or don'tOver time, the run still finishes and the chest still opens, smaller. Only the key's owner drops 1. Nobody else loses anything for helping.

Each level

Every level adds two monster levels, 10% health and 5% damage, on top of the Black Crypt's numbers (its boss is level 65). So Mythic 10 is a level-85 Hoodie, Mythic 20 is a level-105 one, and Mythic 30 is level 125, the top of the game. There's no cap: the Hall of Mythics decides how far it goes.

Twists

Eight, and a new set every Thursday, announced in the weekly issue on The Road Ahead. One twist from Mythic 4, two from Mythic 7, and from Mythic 10 a third from the hard list. Every twist has an answer somebody can bring, so the week's twists decide who you want in the party.

What it pays

Mythic pays in things, not tickets, the way the Pyramid already does. The three paid clears a day stay three, at the normal ticket rate, so Mythic can never become the only sensible way to earn. What climbs with the level is the chest.

The three choices are rolled when the vault opens and scale with your best level: shards at 5, a boss piece at 10, a Primordial gem at 15, a chase roll at 20. One run in time is enough to fill it, so nobody has to grind every day.

The Hall of Mythics

A board per dungeon, reset every Thursday: the highest level beaten in time, then the fastest. It sits on the hiscores page beside the skills. The week's top party gets a line from CASINO, and anybody who finishes a season in the top ten keeps a title for it.

Titles"Keyholder" for a Mythic 10 in time, "Mythic" at 15, "Mythic Legend" at 25, and "Season's Best" for the top party each season.
A lookFrom Mythic 20 your dungeon gear gets a faint violet edge for the season; the Juggernaut's and Mender's auras from mockup 2 get the same edge.
SeasonsTwelve weeks. Keys go back to 1 at the start of each, the Hall keeps last season's top ten on a second tab.

The Tower's climb

The Tower is solo, so it gets a solo version. The Mythic climb is floors 100 and up, past the House: the Tower already works out every floor from a level, so those floors cost nothing to build. The week's twists apply, there's no key and no clock, and a death ends the climb. The board is the highest floor reached this week.

Why no clockThe Tower was built to be the calm one: long fights, semi-AFK. Its Mythic keeps that and asks a different question, "how far before you slip?", instead of "how fast?".
What it paysA shard every five floors and a Vault pick of its own on Thursday, by your best floor. Tickets as the Tower pays now.

Building it

  • A key is two numbers per player (Crypt, Pyramid) and a best floor for the Tower. The run's level multiplies the existing tier numbers when the private copy is made; the dungeons' monsters are already made per tier, so this is one more tier made on the fly.
  • The clock is a start time on the run and a check at the boss's death. The page draws the strip from the start time it's told once, so there's no new poll.
  • Twists are hooks the combat code mostly has: a rise on death (the Pyramid's burrow), an enrage below a fraction (the Tower's), fire patches (the Ice Man's slam marks), heal multipliers. Cursed needs the Mender's Cleanse from mockup 2. The week's set is picked by the date, so the server and the page agree without a message.
  • The Vault and the Hall are one row per player a week, written at the run's end and read only when someone opens them.
  • Mythic 1-9 needs nothing else, so it can ship before Ascendancies, with the role checks switched on when healers exist.
Things to decide: the clock (12 minutes for the Crypt, 15 for the Pyramid, from today's clears); whether roles start at Mythic 10 and 15 or later (36 players is a small pool for four-person parties with set roles); twelve-week seasons with keys reset, or never reset; and whether The House's Cut is funny or annoying.
