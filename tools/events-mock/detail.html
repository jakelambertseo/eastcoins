🌠 Shooting Stars

A star falls somewhere in the world every few hours. CASINO gives a warning and then the exact spot. Everyone who turns up mines it down tier by tier. The top tiers need high Mining, the bottom ones anyone, so the best miners start it and the whole server finishes it. Every swing pays stardust, which buys cosmetics and treats at the Star Tent.

⏱ every 3-5 hours, at random🗺 any open outdoor map⛏ Mining 10 to 90👥 better with a crowd, fine alone⌛ ~15 min with a crowd, gone after 60🛡 no risk

How it plays out

From the first warning to spending the dust. Six moments, each a frame of what a player sees.

1Ten minutes out: a streak in the skyA bright streak crosses every outdoor map. CASINO: "A star is falling… it'll come down somewhere in the north in 10 minutes." Only a direction, so people start drifting that way.
2ImpactIt lands with a flash and a shake on that map. CASINO names the map and a landmark ("the Gloam, by the old well"), and a star appears on everyone's world map.
3Tier 8: the first minersIt starts at tier 8, so only Mining 80+ can swing yet. Everyone else sees "Mining 80 needed · you'll be able to at tier 6" and waits nearby. The top miners get the best dust.
4Tier 5: everybody inEach tier breaks with a crack and a "TIER 5!" pop, the star shrinks a size, and more of the crowd can join. The more people swing, the faster it drops.
5Tier 1: spentThe last tier crumbles into sparkles. Everyone who mined sees a summary ("You mined 212 stardust from tiers 6 to 1"), and one lucky swing all day turns up a Starling.
6At the Star TentBack in the Yard, the Star Tent swaps stardust for a Stardust trail, Starry finishes, gem-roll tokens, star crates and, eventually, a Starling Egg.

Rules and numbers

When and where
  • Every 3 to 5 hours, at random, from the server's clock (the Ice Wyrm's scheduler).
  • Any open outdoor map. Never a dungeon, an island, the casino, or during a raid.
  • It starts at a tier set by who's online: the best Mining online, rounded down (so a quiet night still gets a star someone can open).
Mining it
  • Tier N needs Mining 10×N. Each tier has 60 swings of "health" per miner on it (so it takes about as long with 5 people as with 30).
  • Dust a swing: 1 at tier 1 up to 6 at tier 9. Mining xp: about the best rock of that level, ×1.2.
  • 400 stardust per star each, at most. A swing from a pickaxe; the usual 3-minute idle rule applies.
What it's worth
  • A whole star from tier 6 down is ~200 dust. The Stardust trail is ~10 stars, the Starling Egg ~125.
  • Starling: 1 in 6,000 swings (roughly one a day across the server).
  • Tickets never come out of it directly, so it's a new currency and never inflates the old one.
When it goes wrong
  • Nobody comes: after 60 minutes it fades ("The star cools and goes dark").
  • A restart: the star is saved like the Wyrm and comes back where it was.
  • Lands in the Wilderness (1 time in 8): double dust, and the PvP risk that comes with it. CASINO says so loudly.

📜 Wanted!

On the hour, a poster goes up on the Bounty Board in the Yard, and CASINO reads it out. The target is a named, tougher version of a real monster on a real map, marked with a red WANTED plate. Everyone who hurts it splits the bounty by damage, with the open bosses' after report. You have an hour; if it gets away, the next bounty is bigger.

⏱ every hour, on the hour🎯 one target at a time⚔ any style, any level can chip in💰 5,000 to 50,000, shared⌛ 60 minutes to catch it🔁 escaped → next bounty +25%

How it plays out

1On the hour: a new posterThe Bounty Board in the Yard gets a fresh poster. CASINO reads it out: the name, the map, the bounty. The board itself is clickable any time.
2The huntIt wanders its map like any monster, just bigger, with a red plate and a WANTED tag, and a red glow you can see from across the map. The world map marks the map, not the tile.
3The fightSwords, bows and spells all count. It has several times the normal health, more with a crowd. At half health CASINO says so ("Old Tom is half dead. Six hunters on him").
4Enraged at 25%One simple twist per target. Old Tom charges in a straight line, telegraphed with red tiles like the Foundry's slams. Get out of the way or get flattened.
5TakenIt drops, the poster in the Yard is stamped TAKEN, and everyone who hurt it gets the report and their share, plus a roll on its map's rare drops at five times the usual chance.
6…or it got awayAt the hour it slinks off. The poster is stamped ESCAPED, and the next bounty is 25% bigger (it stacks, up to double). CASINO rubs it in.

Rules and numbers

The poster
  • On the hour, every hour. The level band rotates (low, mid, high) so every level gets one within three hours.
  • Only maps somebody online can fight on. Never a dungeon or an island.
  • 60 minutes to catch it. The poster, the tracker and the map pin all say where.
The target
  • That map's monster, named, 6× health (+10% per hunter after five), hits a little harder, and red-glowing so it can be spotted.
  • One twist at 25%, telegraphed. It never leaves its map.
  • Same weaknesses as its kind, so the usual style advice holds.
The pay
  • Bounty by level band: 5,000 low, 20,000 mid, 50,000 high. Escaped: next one +25% (up to double).
  • Shared by damage, with a floor of 3% of the bounty for anyone who landed a hit.
  • Every hunter rolls its map's rare drops at 5× chance. Ten takes: the Bounty Hunter title.
When it goes wrong
  • One person kites it forever: it can't; it heals slowly if nobody's hit it for 30 seconds.
  • A restart mid-hunt: the poster is saved; the target is put back with its health.
  • Nobody online at that level: the band skips to one somebody can fight.

💰 The Jackpot Thief

Out of nowhere a goblin grabs a sack of the Jackpot and bolts out of the casino. He's quick and slippery, and every hit knocks coins out of his sack onto the ground for whoever grabs them first. Every minute he dives down a hole to another map, and CASINO shouts where. The last hit takes what's left in the sack. If he gets away after five hops, it all goes back to the Jackpot.

⏱ 2-4 times a day, at random🏃 a 5-minute chase across maps🪙 every hit spills coins, anyone's💰 last hit: the rest of the sack🔁 escapes → back to the Jackpot🛡 costs nobody anything

How it plays out

1The heistThe Jackpot pill on the casino floor flashes red and drops ("-8,400 STOLEN"). CASINO: "STOP, THIEF!" He's out the front doors and into the Yard.
2The chaseHe runs away from whoever's nearest, zig-zagging. He's a bit faster than you walk, so a crowd can box him in where one person can't.
3Every hit spills coinsA hit knocks a spray of coins out of the sack onto the ground: tickets anyone can grab. Scramble for them or keep chasing? That's the choice.
4Dizzy!Every tenth hit he stumbles, seeing stars, for three seconds: the moment the crowd catches up and piles in.
5The hopEvery minute he dives down a hole and pops up on another map. CASINO: "He's in the Boneyard!" The tracker and the world map follow him.
6Caught!The last hit bursts the sack: a fountain of coins for the one who landed it, and a big CASINO call. (Or, after five hops, he escapes and the sack goes home.)

Rules and numbers

When
  • 2 to 4 times a day, at random, only with 5+ people online. Never during a raid or the King.
  • He starts at the casino's front doors and runs into the Yard.
The sack
  • 5% of the Jackpot, between 2,000 and 15,000.
  • Each hit spills 1.5% of the starting sack as a pile of ground tickets, first to click takes it (anyone, not just the hitter).
  • The last hit gets whatever's left. Escaped: the rest goes back in the Jackpot. So the Jackpot is only ever lent.
Him
  • A small health pool that refills each hop, but big enough that it takes a crowd about a minute.
  • 1.3× your walking speed, runs from the nearest player, can't be cornered in a doorway forever (he vaults).
  • Dizzy for 3 s every 10 hits. Hops every 60 s to a random open map with people on it; 5 hops and he's gone.
When it goes wrong
  • He hops into the Wilderness: allowed, once per chase. Coins there are PvP loot like anything else.
  • A restart: the chase ends and the sack goes back to the Jackpot.
  • Bots vacuuming coins: a pile needs a click and you must be within 2 tiles.

🗓️ A day of events

How the three would share a day with what's already there. Wanted every hour is the heartbeat, stars and the thief are surprises, and nothing big ever lands on top of anything else.

Red: a Wanted poster each hour. Gold: a star (lasts up to an hour). Purple: a thief chase (five minutes). Blue: what already runs (the open bosses' respawns and the Ice Wyrm). The red line is now. Stars and thieves are drawn at random each day, so this is one example day.

The rules between them
  • Never two "come here now" events in the same 20 minutes: a star won't land within 20 min of the thief or the King.
  • Nothing during a Yard raid except Wanted (which carries on).
  • All of it through CASINO, so chat is where people hear about everything.
What it costs to run
  • No admin needed: the server's clock runs all three.
  • Admin buttons for each (start now, stop), like the raid and the Wyrm.
  • A /events command: what's on now and what's coming.
