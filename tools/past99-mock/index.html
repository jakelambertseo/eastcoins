The Road Past 99

Every skill goes on to 120. Ninety-nine stays the badge it is today; the twenty-one levels after it are the long game: months for the most dedicated, a reason to keep training a skill you have already "finished", and something worth showing on a profile. Every fifth level unlocks something real for that skill, so the climb is never twenty-one empty levels. This is mockup 1 of the long-game set: Ascendancies (2) and endgame gear and crafting (3) hang off it.

The curve: how long is 120?

Level 99 is 13.0M XP today, on RuneScape's own curve (each level about 10% more than the last). There are three sensible ways on to 120, and the gentlest is already twice the whole road to 99: even if every level past 99 cost no more than 99 itself did, that is twenty-one of them. The hours use what players actually put in: the most active put XP an hour into their main skill across all their time online (median of the regulars ), and a "focused" pace of 100k an hour, which is a guess at someone grinding the best spot at 100+.

Recommendation: RuneScape's curve, continued. It is the honest extension of the curve players already know, and 120 becomes a real achievement: at today's top pace it is a year of play; focused, about a thousand hours. What keeps that from feeling hopeless is everything below: a real unlock every five levels, a gold number from the first level past 99, and better resources at 110 that raise the XP an hour. If that is too far, the "three times" curve gets to 120 in well under half the time with the same unlocks, and the dial can be moved later without anyone losing a level (levels are read from XP, so a gentler curve only ever moves people up).

How it looks in the game

Past 99 a skill turns gold-on-violet in the Skills tab, its number keeps going, and the bar fills toward the next level as now. Every fifth level gets the big banner with what it unlocked. The first person to 120 in any skill gets a CASINO line and a permanent plaque on the hiscores.

All 18 skills, 100 to 120

One unlock at 100, 105, 110, 115 and 120. The pattern holds across skills, so a player learns it once: 100 is a small boost you feel at once, 105 a convenience, 110 is the big one (a new top resource or recipe, and the materials for the Relic gear in mockup 3), 115 a second boost, and 120 the cape. The combat skills are different: their levels past 100 are points for the Ascendancy tree (mockup 2).

Building it

  • The curve is a longer XP table (21 more numbers) and every place that stops at 99 learning to stop at 120: the level function, the Skills tab, the hiscores, the profile and the wiki. Levels come from XP, so nobody's saved character changes.
  • Requirements below 100 are untouched: everything that needs 90 still needs 90. Only the new 110 resources need 110.
  • The 90 unlocks are mostly numbers on systems that already exist (double gathers, speed, rare-find, reforge success). The five 110 resources and recipes are new items, each with PixelLab art, and they are the inputs for mockup 3.
  • The cape slot is new: one more equipment slot, eighteen capes and a Max Cape, each with art.
  • Cost on the server: none worth mentioning. A level is a lookup in a slightly longer table.
  • Order: the curve and the Skills tab first (so nobody is ever stuck at a 99 that should have kept going), then the unlocks a skill at a time, then the capes.
