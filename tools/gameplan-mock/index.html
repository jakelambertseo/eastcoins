The game plan

Nine mockups, read together. This page says what the numbers say about them, what has to change before any of it is built, what none of them cover, and the order to build them in. Internal only. Everything here is measured against the server as it is on 1 October 2026: 36 regular players, twelve days since the reset, the best fighter at Melee 85, and the best XP pace ever measured here, 35,750 an hour.

The nine on one board

Each one in a line: what it needs first, who it's for today, what it costs, and the verdict. "Works at" is the level a player has to be for the thing to matter at all, and it is the column that decides the order.

The clock

The one fact that orders everything: how far away 99, 110 and 120 actually are. Hours of play at the best pace ever measured on this server (35,750 XP an hour into one skill), at the median regular's pace, and at a "focused" 100,000 an hour that the 110 resources are supposed to make possible. The calendar assumes six hours a day, which is what the top player has done since the reset.

The top three hit 99 in about six weeksThen the game stops: levelOf caps at 99 today. Mockup 1 isn't a nice-to-have for December, it's the wall the best players walk into. It has to be the first big thing built.
110 is March, 120 is 2028On the RuneScape curve to 120 the best player needs ~2,800 more hours. The Relic tier (3) and the four maps (4) are built for players who won't exist for five months, and for most of the server not inside the year.
So most of what we build next is for levels 1 to 99Diaries, pets, Mythic 1-9, the stats window, the abilities, a weekly boss: everything in the nine that works for everyone goes first. The 110+ content goes in the order the top players will reach it, and not before.

Build order

Maps first: see the atlas.The atlas (the next page in the nav) measured every skill's content and lays out where each one goes to 120. These phases now carry its maps: the held Orchard Wall and Foundry open in phase 1, the two gateways land with levels to 120 in phase 2, then the Elderwood, the Underforge, the Deep End with the High Strip, and the Star Archive.

Seven phases, each a thing people can feel the week it lands. The rule for the order: value to the whole server now, divided by cost, and nothing before the players who'd use it exist. Effort is working weeks of the two of us; art is the PixelLab bill in pieces that need animating (a monster is four facings plus walk, attack and death, and it's the cost that dominates everything else).

The roadmap

October 2026 to September 2027, with the clock's projections marked so each phase lands before the players it's for. Internal only: The Road Ahead keeps teasing one thing at a time, as it does now.

The dates past December are honest guesses with one person building and one person deciding; the ORDER is the part to hold onto. A phase that slips pushes the ones after it; nothing jumps the queue except a balance fix.

Decisions that block building

The forty-odd "things to decide" across the nine, cut down to the ones that change what gets built. Each has my pick.

Principles

Build for 36, not for 3Every phase has to give the median player something that week. The top three get the road past 99 on time, and that's their share.
One communityNo guilds, no tags, no factions. Anything "social" is the whole server doing one thing together, the way the raids already are.
Events are the hitEach phase ends with something CASINO can narrate: a weekly boss, a first Elder, a first Mythic 10, a first 100.
No new skillsEighteen is the count. Everything new is a use for a skill that exists, which is how sockets went to the Gem Sorter.
Custom art, stagedEverything ships with its own PixelLab art, but an Elder without its art yet keeps its Prime picture and the gold ring, and a map ships with its first three monsters before its seventh.
Say the request count out loudBefore anything new polls or saves, its requests a day × players is written down. Nothing in the nine needs a new poll; two things (pet XP, diaries) ride events that already happen.
Tested on Node's SQLite firstEvery path that moves XP, tickets or an item gets a test like the Flood's and the crate's before it meets a player.
A wiki page and a weekly issueEach release gets its pages the same day, and the Thursday issue on The Road Ahead tells it.
