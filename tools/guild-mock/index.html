Guilds

Small groups of friends with a name, a banner, a hall they build together and a boss of their own every week. Sized for this server: about 36 regular players, so guilds are small (3 to 15), easy to start, and optional. Nothing in combat or skilling needs one, and nobody outside a guild is worse off. What a guild gives you is people to play with, a place to put things and a reason to log in on the same evening.

Starting one

Bom sells a guild charter. Three players sign it, and it becomes a guild: a name, a three-letter tag by every member's name, and a banner from two colours and an emblem. Try it:

3 to 15 membersThree to found, so a guild is always a group; fifteen at most, so the server holds a few guilds rather than one that everybody has to join.
Leader, officers, membersThe leader and officers invite, promote and kick; members do everything else. If the leader is away 30 days, the longest-standing officer takes over.
One at a time, easy to leaveYou can be in one guild. Leaving is instant; joining another waits a day, so nobody hops guilds to collect bosses.
A name everyone seesThe tag sits before your name in the world and in chat, in the banner's colour. The banner hangs on your island's flagpole and over the hall door.

The hall

Every guild gets a hall: a private map, like an island, with a portal in the Yard to reach it. It starts as an empty room with a bank chest and the boss's arena door, and the guild builds it up with projects, the same shape as Sal's: everyone chips in materials and tickets, tier by tier. Perks stay inside the hall, so a guild is a nicer place to be, never a stronger character.

The weekly boss

Every Thursday a boss moves into each guild's arena, one of four on a rotation. Its health lasts the whole week: damage stays when you leave, so a guild whose members play at different hours still gets there together. Its health scales with how many members played last week. The first three quarters can be chipped away alone, whenever you like. The last quarter is the finish: the boss locks the arena until three members are in it together, and then it has to die within 15 minutes or it heals back to 25%.

The finish opens at 25%. From there the arena door stays shut until three members are inside together; then it's 15 minutes to kill it. Guests from outside the guild can make up the three (see below).
What everyone getsAnyone who did 1% or more opens the boss's chest: tickets, Relic shards from mockup 3 at combat 100+, a boss trophy for the hall the first time, and a 1 in 200 chance of the boss's pet. 5% and 15% each add a roll.
A level for everyoneThe boss's level matches the guild: it fights at the average combat level of whoever is in the arena, so a guild of level-50s and one of level-110s both have a fair week.
Never a choreMissing a week loses nothing. There's no streak, no penalty, and the boss leaves quietly at the reset.

Without a guild

Nothing is lockedEvery map, skill, item, raid, dungeon and event works the same outside a guild. The hall's perks only work in the hall; the boss is the only thing a guild has that you don't, and it's a weekly extra, not a source of gear.
GuestsA guild can invite anybody to help with its finish. A guest counts towards the three, gets a small guest's chest (tickets and a shard), and can be a guest in more than one guild a week. A good way for a guild to meet people.
Finding oneA "Looking for a guild" switch on your profile, and a list of guilds that are recruiting on the guild board, with their tag, banner, size and the hours they play.

Building it

  • A guild is one record in the game server's storage: name, tag, banner, members and ranks, the bank, the hall's project tiers and this week's boss health. A player carries only their guild's id.
  • The hall is an instanced map the way an island is, made when someone walks in and thrown away when it's empty. Its projects are Sal's project code pointed at the guild instead of the world.
  • The boss is one monster whose health is saved to the guild record on every hit's tick (as the raid saves itself since the restart fix), so it survives leaving, logging out and a server restart. The finish's three-together lock is the Crypt's "everyone in the antechamber" lever.
  • Guild chat is sent only to online members. The roster's online dots are read from the players already in memory when the window opens: no poll.
  • Art: the four bosses (with walk, attack and death), the hall's ground and furniture, the charter, banners as drawn shapes with emblems from the game's own icons.
Things to decide: the size (3 to 15; with 36 players, should the cap be 10?); whether the boss is the same level for every guild or matched to the members, as here; guests in the finish; whether a guild hall should have any perk that works outside it (my pick: no); and the charter's price.
