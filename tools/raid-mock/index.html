Yard raids: three more

The Ice Man worked because everybody could take part, CASINO told the story, and the whole crowd split one pot. These three keep all of that and each adds one new thing to do: chase (the Heist), dodge (the Siege Engine) and build (the Flood). All three keep to the west bank, so the court stays open, and all three survive a restart the way the Ice Man now does. The pictures are the real Yard; the raid is drawn over it in the kit.

💰 The Heist

New: chase10 minutesAll levelsReuses the Jackpot Thief

A crew cleans out the Casino's back room and comes out through the Yard with the sacks, splitting up for every way out at once. Nobody has to tank a boss: you pick a runner and run them down. Every runner carries sacks; every hit knocks tickets onto the grass for whoever is closest; a runner who is stopped drops the lot. The crew's boss, Fat Tony, waits at the bridge with the getaway cart and only comes out for the last two minutes.

💥 The Siege Engine

New: dodge12 minutesAll levelsTelegraphed tiles

A war machine rolls up the north road and parks at the top of the Yard: the Grinder. Every few seconds it lobs a boulder, and the tiles where it will land glow red with a count-down first. Stand in them and you take a third of your health; step out and you take nothing. Its crew of Sappers keeps patching it, so somebody has to keep the crew off while somebody else breaks the engine. It is the first fight in the game where where you stand matters more than what you wear.

What it takes to buildone genuinely new piece
  • Telegraphed tiles already exist: the Ice Man's slam paints red tiles (TILEFX). The boulder is that, on a timer, landing 3 seconds later.
  • The engine is a monster that never moves, with a weak point behind it and a repair tick from its crew.
  • The worksite's health is a number on the raid, drawn on Bronny's board.
  • Art (PixelLab): the Grinder (idle, firing, broken), a Sapper with a wrench, a boulder and its impact.
  • Cost: one volley every 6 seconds is a handful of tile checks. Cheaper than a wave.

🌊 The Flood

New: build15 minutesSkillers welcomeThe water spreads

The river bursts its banks. Water creeps over the Yard a tile at a time, and the Drowned climb out of it. Fighting them only slows the flood; what stops it is sandbags: marked spots along the bank that take logs, stone and sand, handed in by anyone. It is the first raid a woodcutter or a miner can win as much as a fighter. Every sandbag spot finished pushes the water back off the tiles behind it.

What it takes to buildthe biggest of the three
  • Spreading water is the new piece: a set of tiles that grows from the river, slows walking, blocks gathering and is drawn as an overlay. The map grid already changes on a clock (the agility gates do it every tick).
  • Sandbag spots take hand-ins like Bronny's order does.
  • The Undertow is a boss in the pond, hit from the bank.
  • Art (PixelLab): the Drowned (walk, attack), the Bank Shark on land, the Undertow, a sandbag pile at three stages, a water tile.
  • Cost: the water re-checks the walkable tiles every 10 seconds, not every tick: cheap, but new.

Suggested order: the Heist first (smallest, reuses the Thief, and the low levels finally get a raid they can carry), then the Siege Engine (dodging is the mechanic the game is missing), then the Flood once those two have shown how often people want a raid. Each one rotates with the Ice Man rather than adding a raid on top of him, so the Yard sees about the same number of raids a day.
