The two "island" skills, taken out into the world. Thieving lives in one building today and Agility in one lane, and neither gives anything to anyone else. The Guild's marks

A pick succeeds at min(90%, 55% + 2% × (your level − the mark's) + steal), takes 2.4 s, and a fail stuns you 2-3.5 s and loses one stolen good from your bag. Every fifth clean lift in a row pays a second roll. The Ditched set, the Lockpick Set, potions and two pets add steal, capped at +15%.

The recommendation

Keep the Guild as the gym. Take Thieving out into the world on two hooks: pockets and lockboxes.The Guild stays the fastest way to train (indoor, safe, nine marks), so nothing anyone has done there is wasted. Out in the world, people-shaped monsters get pockets (the Highwayman, the Loan Shark, the Deckhand, the Caveman: 19 kinds across 13 maps, no new art) and every map gets one lockbox (one prop each). The world pays less XP an hour than the Guild but better loot: keys, set pieces and a pinch of the monster's own drops. That gives every map a reason for a thief, which was the atlas's complaint ("Thieving lives in one building"). No new thieving rooms: the Guild only gains two marks to fill its gaps. Note: the atlas picked "the Guild to 97, the maps past 100"; this page moves the maps earlier, because the world hooks cost almost nothing and the island problem is now, not at 100.

Pockets in the world

Any monster with a pocket line in its data can be picked, from Thieving 10: the people, never the animals. It's the same pick as the Guild's, on a monster that's just standing there.

  1. Right-click → Pick pocket on a monster that isn't fighting anyone. The menu shows your chance ("Pick pocket · 78%"), worked out exactly as in the Guild with the monster's thieving level.
  2. Success: Thieving XP (about two thirds of a Guild mark at the same level) and one roll on its pocket: the band's fence goods, a key now and then, and rarely one of that monster's own plain drops. The monster is turned out for a minute (a little "empty pockets" puff over it), so one monster can't be farmed faster than walking between them.
  3. Fail: it notices, and you're stunned 2-3.5 s, as in the Guild. Nothing else: no fight, nothing lost (settled 1 October: "it should only stun"). The stun is the whole cost, so the chance in the menu is what matters.
  4. Then kill it if you like. Picking doesn't touch its drops, so "pick, then kill" gets both, and it's why a fighter might want Thieving too.

Lockboxes and keys

One on every mapA strongbox in a corner of each map, at that map's level plus five: the Gloam's at 15 up to the Frozen Reach's at 97. One prop, the same art everywhere with the map's tint.
A lockpick a tryEach try uses a lockpick, 1,000 tickets at Vance (a steady ticket sink, settled 1 October) or made at a Tinkering bench. A try takes 5 s and the same chance as a pick.
Yours, every 15 minutesThe cooldown is per player, so nobody camps a box or races anyone for it. Opened, it pays three pocket rolls at once and a key one time in twenty.
Keys open thingsA skeleton key opens the locked chest at the end of a Crypt, Pyramid or Count run (a fourth roll), or the Guild's back vault. Thieving finally makes something other players want, and keys trade on the Exchange.
Two ways throughSome shortcuts below are a locked gate: climb it with Agility, or pick its lock with Thieving at the same level. Either skill gets you there.
The sets ride alongThe Feller's flannels drop from pockets (1 in 400), as the Sets page planned; the Ditched set's all-four bonus picks a pocket twice one time in ten, out here too.

How a shortcut works

The Guild's level doors already do the hard part: click, the server checks your level, and steps you through a wall that stays a wall for everyone else. A shortcut is that, on a map, with a little crossing animation. Nothing on the map changes for anyone who can't use it.

  1. Hover it: "Fallen log · Agility 10". Below your level it's greyed and the hover says what you need.
  2. Click it: you walk to it, then cross in 1-2.5 s (a hop, a crawl, a swing), and come out on the far side. The server does it, so it can't be cheated, and the walk is drawn like any walk.
  3. XP each crossing (twice the level: 20 at the log, 190 at the ice slide), and a crossing counts toward the Prospector's kit (1 in 150) from the Sets page.
  4. A gentle slip: at exactly the level, one crossing in ten slips and you land back where you started, 2 s lost, no damage. Ten levels over, it never slips. Nothing here hurts.
CuttersWhere the map already makes you walk the long way round. The measurement found five with real detours (the Boneyard saves 46 steps, the Trailer Park 33, the Carnival and the Depths 31, the Boardwalk 26). These are shortcuts in the plain sense.
LedgesMost maps are open fields: the best hop on the Gloam, Sands, Cloudreach, Thunderhead and the Frozen Reach saves 4-6 steps, about a second. So their shortcut leads somewhere you can't otherwise reach: a raised ledge with one extra gathering spot (a tree, a rock or a fishing spot). That's the atlas's "the shortcuts the gathering spots sit behind".
Back waysLater, between maps: a gap in a fence from one map's corner to the next, skipping the long walk through the exits. Worth doing where people run the same two maps back and forth (bank runs).

Every map, measured

For each open map: every pair of walkable tiles two to four apart with something in the way, walked properly with the game's own rules (eight directions, no cutting corners). Drawn: the best hop on each (gold line) and the long way round it saves. A step is 0.2 s at base speed.

Agility's ladder

Shortcuts give Agility a reason; they don't train it. The Run is the only course and has no levels, which is why 1→99 is the slowest of every skill. The fix keeps the same lane and gates:

The aim is Agility 1→99 near Thieving's and the gathering skills' (a target to tune with the skill sim once its Agility model is fixed: it still assumes six gates and a 42-second lap and ignores the runner's marks and the finish bonus). The Getaway silks (+20% Agility XP) and the Grappling Hook (+25%) stack on top.

Balance

The Guild stays fastestWorld pockets pay about two thirds of a Guild mark's XP at the same level, and walking between them costs more. Thieving 1→99 stays about 72 h in the Guild; someone who only picks pockets in the world takes longer but leaves with keys and set pieces.
Can't be farmed faster than walkingA monster is turned out for a minute after a pick; a lockbox is yours once every 15 minutes. The Gloam's six Highwaymen are at most six picks a minute however fast you click.
Still no tickets from pocketsThe rule stands: pockets pay goods you fence. Fence prices don't change. The new faucet is small (fence goods) and the new sink (lockpicks at 1,000) sits right beside it.
A fail only stunsThe tone holds: nothing attacks on sight, and a failed pick never starts a fight. The stun (2-3.5 s) is the cost, the same as in the Guild.
Shortcuts can't trap anyoneEvery shortcut has a way back over it, and the long way round is never closed. A ledge's spots are extra, not moved: nobody loses a rock or a tree they used to reach.
Two skills, one doorWhere a gate takes Agility or Thieving, the level is the same for both, so neither skill is the "real" key.

Building it

  • Pockets: a pocket field on 19 monsters (thieving level, table), one more option in the right-click menu, and the Guild's pick code (the same chance, XP and stun) pointed at a monster. A fail is the Guild's stun, nothing more. About 3 days.
  • Lockboxes: one object type with a per-player cooldown (the pattern of the agility marks, which are already per-player), a lockpick item at Vance, and the keys and the locked chests in the three dungeons. About 3 days and one prop.
  • Shortcuts: one object type on the Guild door's code (check a level, step through), a crossing animation per kind, and for ledges a few tiles of new ground and one extra gathering spot each. Cutters are a day for all five; ledges are the work, about a day a map with the art.
  • The Run's lanes: two more lane settings on the existing course and XP multipliers. A day.
  • The Guild: two marks in its gaps (the 30s and the 80s) and the stale words fixed: the first door says 25 but opens at 10, Vance still says "fifty thousand" for a 15,000 permit, and The Run's quest still says it's in the Gloam.
  • Nothing here changes a bet, the casino, or a ticket price.
Settled 1 October: pockets and lockboxes start at Thieving 10; a failed pick only stuns; a lockpick costs 1,000 tickets; every ledge gets one extra gathering spot, one tier above the map's own (so it's worth the crossing). Still open: The Run's two new lanes.
