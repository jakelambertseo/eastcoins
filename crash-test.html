How the number is picked provably fair

The bust point comes from a seed the server commits to (its hash is shown) before bets open. Bust = max(1, 0.97 ÷ U) where U is uniform from the seed — so 3% of rounds bust at 1.00× and the house edge is 3%. After the round the seed is revealed; hash it yourself and it matches.
