About this mockup

What it would look like to run a second idle game on the Fishing template.

This page is the Fishing game's stylesheet and layout, lifted as they are, with a different game poured into them. Nothing here is saved, nothing here is real, and it is not linked from anywhere. It exists to show how much of Fishing is a template rather than a fishing game.

What would need building for real

The fight is the only genuinely new mechanic — a strike meter read from the clock at the moment of the press, the same way Dead Centre and Field Goal read theirs, so a slow frame can never cost a hit. Everything else is the Fishing code with its tables swapped: species become opponents, rods become weapons, the tank becomes the stable, bait becomes stances and drills, diving loot becomes spoils, and the five bosses become five beasts.
