Rifts of Tartarus

A crack under the arena floor that goes all the way down. Every kill fills the bar, elites fill it faster, and at the top the Guardian comes for you. Each tier is about 12% tougher and drops better gear. Push climbs on a clear and steps back on a death; either way, a death is a rest and another go, so a push runs all night.

Your stable

Gladiators you own fight minor bouts on the undercard and send you their purse, awake or not.

Recruit

Buy a fighter from the slave market. Better stock earns more per hour and can be sent to the tournaments later.

Training yard

Set a drill running and come back to it. Longer drills train more.

Forge

Beat what you win in the arena into something worth carrying back into it.

Gear & loot

Everything you wear dropped off something. Item level caps how good a roll can be; rarity decides how many rolls it gets.

Stash

Whatever the filter doesn't keep is salvaged where it drops: normal into iron, magic into bronze, rare into laurel. Uniques are always kept.

Bosses

One waits at the bottom of every dungeon. Kill it and you keep its title for good.

Passive tree

One class for now: the Warrior. Every level is a point. Spend it on a node joined to one you already hold, and work out towards a keystone.

What your tree gives

The four paths

Brute — damage and strength, ending in Berserker: 40% more damage, 15% less health.

Blademaster — attack speed and crits, ending in Deadly Rhythm.

Juggernaut — health, armour and regeneration, ending in Juggernaut.

Gladiator — accuracy, evasion, fame and loot, ending in Showman's Luck.

Bridges join neighbouring paths, so a build can cross over.

Skills

Your Warrior level comes from every hit you land and feeds the passive tree. Showmanship and Smithing level alongside it.

Market

Food for the dungeons, and the gambler. Your gear comes from what you kill.

The gambler

Gear drops in the dungeons. If you would rather buy a chance at it, the gambler sells a sealed item for any slot, rolled at your combat level. Mostly magic, sometimes rare, very occasionally unique.

How the combat works

It is Melvor's melee maths on OSRS's skills. Your accuracy is (Attack + 9 + style) × (weapon accuracy + 64); an opponent's evasion is (Defence + 9) × 64, and the two give a hit chance. Your max hit comes from Strength and the weapon's strength bonus, and every hit rolls between 1 and it. Each side swings on its own timer. Your health carries from room to room, so food and auto-eat decide how deep you get. Every swing is an event with a timestamp, and the same code plays a live fight and eight hours away, so they can never disagree.
